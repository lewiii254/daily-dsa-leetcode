Before starting, be comfortable with:
- Big-O basics (`O(1)`, `O(n)`, `O(log n)`)
- Arrays and strings basics (indexing, iteration, common operations)

## How to Use This Repo Effectively

For each problem:
1. Read the explanation/README first.
2. Attempt the problem on your own.
3. Review the solution and compare your approach.
4. Note time/space complexity and pitfalls.
5. Re-solve after a day or two without looking.
